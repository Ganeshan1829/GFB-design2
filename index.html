How It Works

Understand how GFB works.

Grinding reduces suitable food scraps into smaller particles that mix with water and pass through the right-side outlet into compatible wastewater drainage.

01

Food waste input

Suitable food scraps enter through the kitchen sink opening and its connection to the machine.

02

Under-sink grinding

The disposer concept reduces suitable scraps into smaller particles before reaching the drainage line.

03

Right-side drainage

Water and particles exit via the separate right-side outlet pipe into compatible wastewater drainage.
